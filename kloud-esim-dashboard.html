Key Observations

⚠ Wage pressure
£8,973 in salaries over 4.7 months consumes 72% of gross profit. Payments are irregular — salary timing causes cash-flow squeeze.
↑ Revenue growing
May (first 21 days) already at £8,900 vs £4,700–5,900 in prior months. If the trend holds, monthly revenue is breaking through a new level.
✕ Thin net margin
Operating profit of ~£244 over 4.7 months is essentially break-even. The £2,000 sponsorship payment in May nearly wiped out the net position in one transaction.
ℹ Owner injections
£996 from Urfan Mahmood Azad (labelled "bills") is personal capital injection, not trading revenue. Removing it, the business barely breaks even on its own cash flow.
⚡ COGS volatile
Cmlink/Firoam purchases vary widely month-to-month (£1,300 in Jan vs £5,200 in May) tracking revenue growth. Margin held consistent at ~43%.
→ Next steps
Salary restructuring or revenue growth to £10k+/month would move the business to sustainable profitability. Commission network (Hajj/Umrah agents) is a scalable channel.
