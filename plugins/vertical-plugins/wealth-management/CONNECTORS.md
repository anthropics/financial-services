# Connectors — Wealth Management

This plugin works in **standalone mode** and is **supercharged with MCP connectors**.

## Core Feature Set (Standalone)

- Client quarterly and annual performance report generation
- Comprehensive financial and retirement planning analysis
- Investment proposal drafting and risk profiling
- Portfolio rebalancing trade calculations and tax-loss harvesting
- Annual client review preparation

## Supercharged Features (with MCPs)

| MCP | Categories | Unlocks | Enhanced Skills |
|-----|------------|---------|-----------------|
| **Morningstar** | `market-data`, `financial-data` | Mutual fund / ETF ratings, asset allocation breakdowns, and fund fee metrics | `portfolio-rebalance`, `investment-proposal`, `client-report`, `client-review` |
| **FactSet** | `market-data`, `financial-data` | Portfolio benchmark returns, asset pricing, and tax-loss harvest cost basis tracking | `client-report`, `financial-plan`, `tax-loss-harvesting` |
| **LSEG** | `market-data`, `financial-data` | Multi-asset quantitative analytics, yield curves, and risk metrics | `portfolio-rebalance`, `investment-proposal` |
