# Connectors — Equity Research

This plugin works in **standalone mode** and is **supercharged with MCP connectors**.

## Core Feature Set (Standalone)

- Comprehensive initiating coverage and thesis tracking notes
- Earnings preview notes and post-earnings reaction analysis
- Sector overview frameworks and catalyst calendar management
- Morning meeting note drafting and trade idea generation
- Financial model update workflows

## Supercharged Features (with MCPs)

| MCP | Categories | Unlocks | Enhanced Skills |
|-----|------------|---------|-----------------|
| **FactSet** | `market-data`, `financial-data` | Real-time equity pricing, IBES consensus estimates, and guidance history | `earnings-analysis`, `earnings-preview`, `model-update`, `morning-note`, `initiating-coverage`, `sector-overview`, `thesis-tracker`, `idea-generation` |
| **S&P Global** | `market-data`, `financial-data`, `company-intelligence` | S&P Capital IQ fundamentals, segment revenue, and company transcripts | `initiating-coverage`, `sector-overview`, `thesis-tracker`, `catalyst-calendar`, `earnings-preview` |
| **Daloopa** | `financial-data` | Detailed line-item consensus, historical actuals, and KPI tracking | `model-update`, `earnings-analysis` |
| **Aiera** | `transcripts` | Live earnings call transcripts, management Q&A analysis, and tone tracking | `earnings-analysis`, `earnings-preview`, `morning-note` |
| **MT Newswires** | `news` | Real-time market news wire feeds and breaking press releases | `morning-note`, `catalyst-calendar`, `idea-generation` |
