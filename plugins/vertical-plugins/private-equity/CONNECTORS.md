# Connectors — Private Equity

This plugin works in **standalone mode** and is **supercharged with MCP connectors**.

## Core Feature Set (Standalone)

- Deal screening, thesis evaluation, and sourcing frameworks
- Investment Committee (IC) memo generation
- Due diligence checklists and management meeting preparation
- Unit economics breakdown and returns analysis (IRR / MoIC)
- 100-day value creation planning and AI readiness assessments
- Portfolio company operational performance tracking

## Supercharged Features (with MCPs)

| MCP | Categories | Unlocks | Enhanced Skills |
|-----|------------|---------|-----------------|
| **PitchBook** | `private-markets`, `company-intelligence` | Private market comps, sponsor transaction benchmarks, and exit multiples | `deal-sourcing`, `deal-screening`, `ic-memo`, `returns-analysis` |
| **Chronograph** | `private-markets` | Portfolio company KPI collection, monthly tracking, and bridge analytics | `portfolio-monitoring`, `value-creation-plan` |
| **S&P Global** | `market-data`, `financial-data`, `company-intelligence` | Comprehensive industry data, private company intelligence, and public comps | `dd-checklist`, `dd-meeting-prep`, `unit-economics`, `ic-memo` |
| **Box** | `document-management` | Data room access, due diligence documentation review, and diligence tracking | `ai-readiness`, `dd-checklist`, `portfolio-monitoring` |
